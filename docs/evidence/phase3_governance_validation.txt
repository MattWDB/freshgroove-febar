=== TABLE COMMENTS APPLIED ===
table_name                   | comment
gold_waste_risk_features     | ML feature table. One row per store/SKU/date with all signals for waste risk scoring. Source of truth for waste_classifier, demand_forecast, and supplier_reliability models.
gold_kpi_summary             | Business KPI summary: avg_waste_rate, total_spoilage_cost_usd, otif_rate, chargeback_cost_usd. Read by app Overview tab and Genie Agent.

=== COLUMN DESCRIPTIONS (documented for Genie space instructions — applied in Session 6) ===
gold_waste_risk_features.spoilage_pct:
  "Fraction of on_hand_units that expired unsold. Formula: spoilage_units / on_hand_units. Target: < 0.06 (6%). Current avg: ~0.12 (12%)."

gold_waste_risk_features.is_high_risk:
  "Binary label: 1 if spoilage_pct > 0.12 (above average), 0 otherwise. Training target for waste_classifier model."

gold_waste_risk_features.sales_7d_avg:
  "Rolling 7-day average units sold for this store+SKU combination. Demand signal for the waste and demand models."

gold_waste_risk_features.spoilage_30d_avg:
  "Rolling 30-day average spoilage rate for this store+SKU. Trend signal — rising rate indicates systemic issue."

gold_waste_risk_features.days_to_season_peak:
  "Days until April 15 seasonal demand peak (strawberry season). Negative = past peak. Used by demand_forecast model."

gold_supplier_risk.late_rate:
  "Fraction of deliveries where units_received < 95% of units_ordered. Rising late_rate indicates unreliable supplier. Threshold for HIGH risk: > 0.20."

gold_kpi_summary.avg_waste_rate:
  "Portfolio-level average spoilage rate across all stores and SKUs. Displayed as 'Waste Rate' KPI in app Overview."

gold_kpi_summary.total_spoilage_cost_usd:
  "Estimated annual spoilage cost in USD. Formula: sum(spoilage_units * $45 unit value). Displayed as 'Spoilage Cost/yr' KPI."

gold_kpi_summary.otif_rate:
  "On-Time In-Full delivery rate. Current: 0.91 (91%). Retailer threshold: 0.98 (98%). Below threshold triggers 3% chargeback penalty."

gold_kpi_summary.chargeback_cost_usd:
  "Estimated annual cost of OTIF chargebacks from retail partners. Current: ~$1.5M/year at 91% OTIF."
