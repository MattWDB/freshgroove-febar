# Value Model — FreshGroove Foods

## Business Case Summary

FreshGroove Foods is losing approximately $3.5M/year across two categories:
- **Perishable waste:** ~$2.0M/year at 12% spoilage rate
- **OTIF chargebacks:** ~$1.5M/year at 91% OTIF

Conservative year-1 target: **$2.2M/year** in combined savings.

## The Math

### Waste Reduction
- 12 stores × 45 SKUs × avg unit cost $45 × 365 days × 12% waste rate = ~$2.0M/year
- Getting waste from 12% to 9% (halfway to 6% benchmark) = $0.5M saved
- Getting waste from 12% to 6% (full benchmark) = $1.0M saved
- **We claim $0.5M–$1.0M/yr depending on adoption speed**

### OTIF Chargeback Reduction
- Annual order value ~$1.5B (from synthetic data)
- 9% non-compliant rate × 3% chargeback penalty = ~$1.5M/year current cost
- Getting OTIF from 91% to 96% = 60% chargeback reduction = **$0.9M/year saved**

### Planner Productivity (supporting evidence only, not in headline)
- 5 planners × 2hr/day exception management × 20% reduction = 200hr/yr saved
- At $75/hr fully loaded = $15K (too small to headline; cited as co-benefit)

## Conservative Headline
**$2.2M/year in year 1.** Up to $3.5M/year at full adoption.
All figures based on synthetic data correlated with industry benchmarks (IRI, Nielsen, FMI).
No real FreshGroove customer data used.
